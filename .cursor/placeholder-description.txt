Chiko Road Path is a skill-based yard puzzle where you plan a hen’s route on a six-by-six board: tap an arrow from the tray, place it on an open cell, then launch Chiko so she walks the path you laid. Every step is fixed by those tiles, with no random turns or luck, and a yard is won only when she visits every feeder and reaches the goal without walking off the edge, hitting a rock, missing feeders, or looping in circles. Twelve yards unlock in order, and the only reward is stars: three for a first try inside the tile par, two for a later try inside par, and one for a win over par, shown on the menu and the yard picker. There is no currency, prize pool, or cash-out, and the game has no in-app purchases.
